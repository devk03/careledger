# Code of Conduct

We want Adeno to be a kind, safe project for caregivers, patients, clinicians, and contributors.

Be respectful, patient, specific, and privacy-conscious. Do not harass, demean, discriminate, expose private health information, or offer false certainty about medical care. Disagreement is welcome when it is evidence-based and humane.

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately through the security contact documented by the project maintainer. Maintainers may remove content or participation that puts people or their data at risk.
